The Business Health Check is a self-assessment tool intended to give directional guidance only. It is not a substitute for a full diagnostic review, which happens as part of an actual engagement.

How engagements work

  • Every engagement begins with a scoped, written proposal covering deliverables, timeline, and price — agreed before work starts.

Confidentiality

We treat information shared with us during an engagement as confidential. A mutual non-disclosure agreement is available on request before any material is shared, and can be signed before a discovery call if you prefer.
