Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Meyvitt collects when you visit this website or contact us, and how we use it. It applies to visitors, prospective clients, and clients.

What we collect

When you use the contact form or email us, we collect what you provide directly: your name, email address, company name (if given), and the content of your message. We do not require you to create an account or provide payment details to contact us.

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may automatically log basic technical information — browser type, approximate location derived from IP address, and pages visited — used only to understand site usage and keep the site working correctly.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the agreed work.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful, so we can improve it.
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations where relevant.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Confidentiality of client work

Any business, financial, or technical information shared with us as part of an engagement is treated as confidential by default. We're happy to sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement before any material is shared, on request.

Where information is stored

Enquiry and client information is stored using standard third-party service providers (such as email and form-processing tools) that maintain their own security practices. We take reasonable steps to limit access to information to the people who need it to do the work.

International visitors

We work with clients based in India, the United States, the United Kingdom, and elsewhere. If you are located outside India, your information may be processed in India or in the country where our service providers operate. By contacting us, you consent to this.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal or contractual reason we may need to retain it (for example, ongoing client work or accounting records). Write to meyvittconsultancy@gmail.com to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
