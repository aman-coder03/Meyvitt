How An Engagement Works

A clear process, agreed upfront.

No open-ended retainers by default. You know what you're getting, by when, and what it costs before we start.

Step 01

Discovery call

30 minutes, no cost. We listen, ask questions, and tell you honestly if we're a fit.

Step 02

Proposal & scope

A written scope, timeline and fixed price. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Step 03

Delivery sprint

Focused work against the agreed scope, with regular check-ins — not silence until the deadline.

Step 04

Handover & support

Documentation, training if needed, and a defined support window after delivery.

Pricing is quoted per engagement in your preferred currency — INR, USD or GBP — and confirmed before any work begins.
